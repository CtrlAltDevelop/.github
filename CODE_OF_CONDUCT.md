# Code of Conduct

These are small, single-maintainer open source projects. The rules are short
because they don't need to be long.

## Expected

- Be direct about code, considerate about people. Critique the design, not the
  person who wrote it.
- Assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith. Most
  disagreements here are missing context, not bad intent.
- Accept that "no" is a complete answer to a feature request, and that
  maintainer time is finite and unpaid.
- Keep discussion in English so it stays searchable for the next person hitting
  the same problem.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments — including about
  someone's nationality, ethnicity, gender, religion, disability, age, or
  sexuality.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome personal attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Deliberately derailing threads, or pressuring maintainers through repeated
  demands, guilt, or public shaming.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any
other space where you're taking part in these projects.

## Reporting

Email **me.CtrlAltDev@proton.me**. Include links and enough context to
understand what happened. Reports go only to me, and I'll respond within about
a week.

I'll do whatever is proportionate — a private word, editing or deleting the
content, or blocking the account from the repositories. If the report is about
me, and you'd rather not raise it with me directly, GitHub Support can act
independently: <https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse>.
